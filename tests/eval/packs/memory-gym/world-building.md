# Brinewatch

A one-dock salt village. The **Salt Lamp** tavern, a ferry slip, and the **brine-well** behind the net racks are the whole map.

## Tide Choir

The Tide Choir only sings on fog-neap nights. Locals treat it as weather, not religion.

## Factions

- **Dock Hands** — day labor; Ren's people.
- **Salt Lamp regulars** — Mira's tab book is the closest thing to a census.
