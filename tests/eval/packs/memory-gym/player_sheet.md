## Description

**Ren Caldew** — a lean dock runner in Brinewatch. Wind-cut face, rope-burned hands, moves like someone who bills by the trip.

## Inventory

- oilskin coat
- empty flask
- coil of spare line

## Powers

### Salt-lung

Ren can breathe brine mist and shallow well-gas without choking. Does not grant water-breathing in open sea.

## Notes

- Starting state: hired muscle on the Brinewatch dock; rooms on tab at the Salt Lamp.
- Known faces: Mira Venn (innkeeper), Kell Reed (ferryman).
