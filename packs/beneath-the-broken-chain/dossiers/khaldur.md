# Khaldur

Tyrian vassal city in the eastern Ringing Mountain foothills. Sandstone steps, hide awnings, quarry dust, pump-chain knock, and Arena stamping. About 18,000 residents live under Malkor Tain.

## Establishment

- The Deep Aquifer is stable. Legal thirst is manufactured through guarded pumps, cisterns, closures, and ration tallies.
- The Arena stands on Rajaat's Green Age temple works, which stand over a distinct Blue Age Ruin.
- Vertical route: Sacrifice Ward → Arena Barracks → Old Work Gate → expired Long Seal → Rajaat's Temple → Blue Age Ruin → Rhulisti Vault.
- The Maintenance Rise reaches the western quarry outside the wall.
- Keth-Tal is a conquered canyon with seepage-fed thorn jungle pockets, Under Toe survivors, and the origin of Malkor's conqueror myth.
