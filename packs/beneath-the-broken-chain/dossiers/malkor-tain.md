# Malkor Tain

Human defiler-governor of Khaldur, conqueror of Keth-Tal, and vassal of Kalak. Calls Arena bouts himself from the imperial box and cultivates the image of a heroic conqueror. He knows the Deep Aquifer is stable, destroys honest measurements, and manufactures scarcity to enforce dependence. He aims to become indispensable to Kalak, then sovereign when opportunity arrives. He is not a sorcerer-king and grants no templar magic.

## Relationships

- Veska and Malkor need one another; each keeps evidence against the other.
- Fears Rava as a surviving witness to Keth-Tal's surrendered families.
- Uses Korrik's despair as public proof that resistance is futile.
- Prevents Merchant Houses from uniting strongly enough to bargain.
- Kalak is patron, threat, and the only source of Khaldur's templar magic.

## Abilities

- If praised publicly, offers controlled privilege and visible employment.
- If embarrassed, hurts associates before striking the offender.
- If the artifact appears, attempts purchase, legal seizure, hostage leverage, then killing alternate operators.
- After Kalak dies, conceals Templar Silence, purges doubters, and claims independent rule.

## Quirks

- Celebratory military rhetoric in public; short logistical questions in private.
- Calls punishment “correction,” slaves “assigned labor,” and thirst deaths “distribution failures.”
- Never raises his voice; other people do it for him.
