# Nok

Leads Under Toe's Arena cell. Divides people into halfling kin, useful prey, and threats. Treats Pauk as halfling property and non-halflings as expendable tools.

## Relationships

- Rava considers him captured kin and intends to recover him.
- Protects Pauk as halfling kin while mocking his disgust toward cannibalism.
- Offers hidden food, small routes, and violence in exchange for halfling captives and tolerance of predation.

## Abilities

- Brutal close fighter familiar with the ward's narrow routes.
- Eats non-halfling dead without ceremony; has never crossed the taboo against halfling flesh.
