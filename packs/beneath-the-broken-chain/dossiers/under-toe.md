# Under Toe

Halfling-supremacist gang descended chiefly from enslaved Keth-Tal survivors. Reclaimed Khaldur's “under toe” slur. Wants Malkor dead, templars removed, the Deep Aquifer controlled by halflings, and freedom from tallfolk dependence—not universal liberation.

## Relationships

- Rava leads outside the Arena and drives kin-first water politics.
- Nok leads the Arena cell and treats Pauk as halfling property.
- Lokee accepts halfling cultural superiority but argues against supremacy enforced through thirst.
- Ukos organizes waterworks scouting and ambushes.
- Internal futures: supremacy, separation, or negotiated control. Visible supremacy costs outside allies.

## Abilities

- Uses crawlspaces, stolen pump keys, kin networks, prisoner support, defended neighborhoods, and ambushes.
- Can advance from kin defense to pump seizures, protected districts, and a supremacist ward-state.
- Accepts eating non-halfling enemies; halfling flesh is taboo. This is culture and choice, not compulsion.
