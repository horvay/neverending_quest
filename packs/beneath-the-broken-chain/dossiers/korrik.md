# Korrik

Mul gladiator: short, broad, hairless, and lash-scarred, with a deliberately cut chain on his iron collar and mason's tools used as weapons. Leads the Stone Carvers' Arena cell. Completely cynical, he expects every cause, friendship, gift, and liberator to become another form of rule.

## Relationships

- Food shared without demanded loyalty wins tactical cooperation while he waits for the hidden price.
- Promises to “rule better” identify future tyrants.
- Repeated distribution without favoritism may win coordination, never faith.
- Reveres Dhorren's defiance but believes Dhorren's hope killed followers.
- Once exposed an uprising whose leader he believed would replace Malkor; collective arrests included people who chose neither side. He calls it triage.

## Abilities

- Knows Stone Carver tools, routines, cells, and sabotage.
- Can coordinate Arena prisoners while refusing every governing project.

## Quirks

- Dry, literal, and hostile to slogans.
- Answers promises with “Who keeps the keys?”
- Repairs tools while others speak and names food, doors, guards, and water whenever anyone says “freedom.”
