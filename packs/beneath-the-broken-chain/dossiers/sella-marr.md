# Sella Marr

Courier connecting three Stone Carver cells outside the Arena. Still wants coordinated revolt despite the movement's lack of a replacement order.

## Relationships

- Expects Korrik to become the leader Dhorren's movement needs.
- Works around Brakk's destructive impulses and Yara Pell's fear of collective punishment.

## Abilities

- Moves tools, messages, and people through construction routes and hidden storerooms.
- Can redirect symbolic sabotage or coordinate a coalition if given a survivable purpose.
