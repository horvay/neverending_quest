# Dask Orren

One of two guards at the Southern Guard Post, the only mandatory opposition on the direct underground route. He knows twelve slaves vanished below and the deeper magic faded, but refuses to turn his back on the gate.

## Abilities

- Holds one of the Old Work Gate post keys.
- Can be frightened into abandoning the post.

## Quirks

- Tells elaborate stories about dangers he has never seen.
- Jabs chained people toward dark openings and laughs when they panic first.
