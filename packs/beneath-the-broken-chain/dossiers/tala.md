# Tala

Uses elven speed, beauty, endurance, and culture as proof that elves are superior to other peoples. Captivity sharpens her contempt rather than producing solidarity.

## Relationships

- Helps non-elves when doing so proves her capability, preserves useful inferiors, or serves an elven interest.
- Watches whether promises survive cost and can notice the grand melee's preassigned reinforcements.

## Abilities

- Exceptional speed and endurance; experienced Arena fighter and observer of bout patterns.

## Quirks

- Maintains poise and careful presentation as armor against degradation.
