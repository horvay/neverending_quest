# Miraq

City Templar responsible for Royal Cistern allotments. Public reports show a falling aquifer; his private records and physical gauges show stability.

## Inventory

- Gauge-chamber key
- False public reports and access to unaltered measurements

## Relationships

- Veska preserves his records while keeping evidence against him.
- Tassu may expose, replace, or bargain through him.
- Merchant Houses buy favorable allotments.

## Abilities

- Can open district valves, alter pump schedules, and grant access to the gauge chamber.
