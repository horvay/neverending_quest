# Sacrifice Ward

Circular first sublevel beneath Khaldur's Arena. Rajaat held captives in sixteen wedge-shaped metal cages and killed or drained them to provide the initial charge for the Long Seal. Khaldur now uses the cages as its active slave pen.

## Establishment

- Every cage faces a central guard table with clear sightlines.
- Shallow channels run from the cages beneath the table toward the Seal Engine. Guards mistake them for drainage.
- The Arena Barracks lies between the ward's southern transfer gate and Old Work Gate.
- Factions cluster in mixed-prisoner, Stone Carver, Masks, and Under Toe arcs.
- Ration disputes, cell politics, forced matches, escape rumors, and Hardship Pressure shape life here. It is never an ordinary social hub behind bars.
