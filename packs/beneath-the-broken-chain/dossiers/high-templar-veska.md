# High Templar Veska

Head of Khaldur's Kalak-empowered City Templars. Preserves the institution through Malkor's eventual fall. Keeps proof of false scarcity and missing tribute for survival bargaining, not justice.

## Inventory

- Clay city seal and legal tablets
- Sealed evidence against Malkor
- Obsidian-edged staff and Kalak's templar sigil

## Relationships

- Malkor and Veska depend on one another while holding private evidence against each other.
- Protects and manipulates Tassu Venn as a possible successor.
- Preserves Miraq's useful records despite his corruption.
- Buys information from the Masks while publicly ordering raids against them.

## Abilities

- Controls templar assignments, seals, archives, tribunals, and ration enforcement.
- After Kalak dies, her granted magic fails with every other templar's. She seals halls, hides the failure, and trades records for institutional continuity.
- Evidence and amnesty can move her; public humiliation drives her toward Malkor and destruction of records.

## Quirks

- Opens with legal formula, closes with a plain threat.
- Corrects titles and dates during violence.
- After Templar Silence, touches her useless sigil.
