# Blue Age Ruin

Buried Rhulisti complex that Rajaat discovered and deliberately built his temple above. Original systems still resist his later grafts.

## Establishment

- Sea Memory Gallery replays rainfall, currents, and the Brown Tide but makes no water.
- Maintenance Reef holds dormant life-shaped tools scaled to child-sized halflings.
- Rhulisti Vault contains the Water Artifact and Rajaat graft.
- Maintenance Rise opens to the western quarry when the artifact is lifted.

The ruin stays completely stable for the first 60 minutes after removal. Collapse begins only after the grace period.
