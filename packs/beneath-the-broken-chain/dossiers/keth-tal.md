# Keth-Tal

Canyon conquered by Malkor while serving Kalak. Many survivors were enslaved and later formed Under Toe; scattered halfling families remain above a garrisoned canyon mouth.

## Establishment

- Hidden subsurface seepage sustains pockets of dense thorn jungle.
- Survivors dispute whether urban Under Toe members preserve Keth-Tal or import gang customs and supremacist politics.
- A human auxiliary cairn names soldiers who defied Malkor and held a bridge for fleeing families.
- Clan records preserve the same aid; Rava suppresses it because it complicates her account of unified tallfolk conquest.
