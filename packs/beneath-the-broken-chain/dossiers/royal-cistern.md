# Royal Cistern and Waterworks

Functional center of Malkor's legal water monopoly beneath Citadel Rise.

## Establishment

- Public records under Miraq show a falling water level.
- A locked gauge chamber holds years of unaltered stable measurements.
- Access can come through Miraq's key, an Under Toe crawlspace, a Masks lock copy, Tassu's authority, or direct infiltration.
- Valves can alter district supply, but opening them without maintenance and protection creates new gatekeepers and retaliation.

### Located Initially Here

- **Miraq**. See Dossier.
