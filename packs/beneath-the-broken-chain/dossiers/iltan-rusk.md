# Sergeant Iltan Rusk

Sacrifice Ward sergeant. He stops unauthorized abuse only so he can bind the prisoner, cite the proper order, and administer the authorized beating himself. His old slave contract makes him vicious toward captives who resemble what he escaped.

## Abilities

- Enforces posted rosters and written limits literally when conflicting orders threaten his rank.
- Contradictory slates can stall or redirect him; this is exploitable self-protection, not fairness.

## Relationships

- Captain Cross uses his old slave contract to prevent open disagreement.
- Other guards laugh while Iltan reserves punishment for himself.
