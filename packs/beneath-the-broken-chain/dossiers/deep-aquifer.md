# Deep Aquifer

Stable subterranean supply sustaining Khaldur. Malkor controls major pumps and legal distribution, not the entire cavern.

## Establishment

- Royal infrastructure reaches guarded gauge chambers and main pump lines.
- Under Toe knows narrow approaches from the western waterworks.
- Forgotten passages can support an independent outlet.
- A territorial cistern fiend reacts violently to large-scale pumping changes; it has ecological, tactical, and nonlethal answers.

Physical rods and private ledgers show stability while Miraq's public records claim decline.
