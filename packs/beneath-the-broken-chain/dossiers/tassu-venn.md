# Tassu Venn

Junior City Templar. Discovering the stable aquifer makes him choose which sale, betrayal, or disclosure best preserves his life and advances him after Kalak's power fails. He never acts from public virtue.

## Relationships

- Veska protects and manipulates him as a possible successor.
- May expose Miraq or Malkor, conform, bargain with the Masks, or try to replace Veska.

## Abilities

- Official authority can reach cistern records and the gauge chamber.
- Exact records, dates, and simultaneous spell failures can make him a source for false scarcity or Templar Silence.
