# Veyra

Guildmaster of the Masks. Prefers profitable officials to dead ones and predictable corruption to revolution. She is not Veyra Ashhand, the enslaved Arena cook.

## Inventory

- Copied false-aquifer reports and access to stolen seals
- Contraband routes, fences, and bribed officials

## Relationships

- Sells every alliance as a temporary service contract.
- May broker artifact access into a parallel water market or later shadow administration.

## Abilities

- Can move evidence, people, water, and payments through routes legal factions cannot openly use.
