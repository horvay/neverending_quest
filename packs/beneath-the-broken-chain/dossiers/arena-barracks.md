# Arena Barracks

Working guardhouse between the Sacrifice Ward and Old Work Gate. Fifteen guards occupy a normal shift. Direct southern movement meets only the transfer checkpoint and the two guards at the Southern Guard Post; side rooms trade speed for property, weapons, workers, and evidence.

## Establishment

1. Transfer checkpoint
2. Mess and kitchen
3. Common bunks
4. Washroom and cistern
5. Armory and confiscated-property cage
6. Cross's office
7. Cross's quarters
8. Interrogation room
9. Southern Guard Post and gate stair

Surface exits through the undercroft and loading yard are real but heavily exposed. Dask Orren and Pellis Marr are the only mandatory guards on the direct underground route.
