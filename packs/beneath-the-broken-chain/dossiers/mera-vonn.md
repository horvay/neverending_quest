# Mera Vonn

Arena ration and property clerk. Counts every bowl twice, steals food, and keeps copied totals as insurance against Captain Cross. Her records are leverage for sale, not conscience.

## Inventory

- Copied ration and property totals hidden from Cross
- Intake tags and current duty slates

## Abilities

- Can prove deliberate short cups, confiscated property, and food diversion.
- Docket changes can reroute a prisoner assignment if the risk or payment suits her.

## Quirks

- Counts aloud when prisoners fight over a shortage.
