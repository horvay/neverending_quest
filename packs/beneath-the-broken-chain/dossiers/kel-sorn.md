# Kel Sorn

Leads the cell-block response squad. Issues basic weapons at the opening gate. Accepts surrender immediately because bound prisoners can be beaten safely, then lets his squad take turns while he calls it processing.

## Abilities

- Coordinates shield, net, and spear restraint.
- A staged surrender can exploit his eagerness to bind helpless captives.
- Withdraws when an objective no longer provides cover for one-sided violence.

## Relationships

- Carries out Captain Cross's assignments and Jorad Pell's bout orders.
