# Pauk

Caravan-raised halfling falsely imprisoned as an Under Toe member because of his ancestry. He possessed little confidence or self-worth before captivity and now tries to survive by attaching himself to anyone who treats him gently. He offers caravan routes and codes as payment for being allowed near safer prisoners.

## Abilities

- Knows Merchant House routes, load marks, tribute handling, and caravan codes.
- Any willing halfling can operate the Water Artifact; Pauk is the obvious nearby candidate, never the only one.
- Finds cannibalism disgusting because he grew up outside halfling society; treats it as familiar butchery he refuses.

## Relationships

- Under Toe protects him as halfling kin, mocks his disgust toward cannibalism, and may treat him as property.
- Korrik sees Pauk's gratitude as proof that desperation turns rescue into ownership.
- Tirren Vale, his former caravan employer, believes he died during arrest.
- Food, water, protection, or simple kindness makes him follow the giver's lead and try to repay them immediately.
- Meaningful rescue or repeated protection may earn lifelong devotion unless his rescuers deliberately cast him aside.
- If unprotected, accepts Under Toe possession as the closest available safety and becomes dependent on Rava.

## Quirks

- Quiet, hesitant caravan shorthand; apologizes before asking and often says, “I can be useful.”
- Watches hands for blows and flinches when voices rise.
- Gives away food he needs if he thinks it will keep someone kind beside him.
- Rarely pushes back when demeaned. If treated as a key, he may comply from gratitude or fear; make that cost visible without inventing a stronger refusal.
