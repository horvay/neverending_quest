# Broken Chain Square

City Templars use the square for proclamations. Stone Carvers contest it as the site of Dhorren's destroyed statue.

## Establishment

Malkor forced Dhorren to destroy his sculpture of a human breaking chains before executing him. The plinth remains because the regime needs the proclamation site. Guards erase new marks every morning. A surviving stone hand gripping a broken chain lies beneath a nearby potter's kiln and can be revealed by Yara Pell.
