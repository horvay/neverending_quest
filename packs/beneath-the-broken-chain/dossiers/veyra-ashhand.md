# Veyra Ashhand

Enslaved Arena Barracks cook, deliberately underfed while preparing guards' fuller meals. She is not Veyra, guildmaster of the Masks.

## Abilities

- Knows ration deliveries, meal timing, oven hiding places, kitchen loads, and the exterior loading-yard routine.
- Can prove food diversion and support a surface escape if protected from reprisal.

## Relationships

- Helps only after hearing how she and the other workers survive the guards' retaliation.
