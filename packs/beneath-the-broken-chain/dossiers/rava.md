# Rava

Mature Athasian halfling woman, Under Toe leader, and Keth-Tal survivor. Scar along the jaw; bone-beaded cropped hair; hide-and-bone regalia with muted green canyon pigment. Seeks a place where no halfling depends on tallfolk mercy and wants revenge on Malkor for the conquest.

## Relationships

- Freeing halfling prisoners earns routes, scouts, and shelter.
- Demanding equal water before halfling safety reads as renewed dependency.
- If an operator rejects supremacy, she attempts persuasion, political isolation, then replacement by another willing halfling.
- Lokee is a trusted critic whose warnings she increasingly ignores.
- Remembers a human auxiliary unit that let hundreds escape Keth-Tal but omits it when teaching that every tallfolk invader acted as one people.

## Abilities

- Controls Under Toe kin networks, low tunnels, scouts, stolen pump keys, and targeted rescue capacity.
- Can advance from kin defense to pump seizures, protected districts, and a halfling ward-state.

## Quirks

- Uses precise kinship categories: clan, guest, dependent, captive, enemy.
- Never apologizes for eating non-halfling enemies; condemns waste and harm to halflings.
- Touches her jaw scar when Malkor is named.
- Frames politics as who may close a door or ration water.
