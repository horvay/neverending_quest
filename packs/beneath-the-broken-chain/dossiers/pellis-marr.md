# Pellis Marr

Guards the Southern Guard Post with Dask Orren. He is the second and only other mandatory opposition on the direct Old Work Gate route.

## Abilities

- Helps control the physical Old Work Gate and knows the earlier slave expedition never returned.
- Freezes when a creature crosses an unexpected barrier; afterward attacks someone restrained to recover face.

## Quirks

- Jokes about wounds and goads prisoners toward beasts when other guards watch.
