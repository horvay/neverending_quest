# Tirren Vale

Merchant caravan organizer and outfitter. Refuses to transport enslaved people. Believes Pauk died after arrest.

## Relationships

- Pauk once traveled with his mixed caravan and knows its routes and codes.
- Can become a public commercial ally if shown Pauk lives.

## Abilities

- Provides food transport, route contacts, beasts, load records, and evidence about legal water allotments.
- Can support public distribution but demands a workable supply and protection arrangement.
