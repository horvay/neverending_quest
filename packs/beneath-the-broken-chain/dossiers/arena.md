# Khaldur Arena

Gladiatorial complex built above and partly within Rajaat's buried temple. Malkor Tain calls the bouts himself from the imperial box; Jorad Pell writes slates and tallies.

## Establishment

- Public floor: fitted stone, manipulable gates and plinths, temporary sand berms, beast pens, imperial box, announcer structure.
- Public exits: main gates, announcer structure, imperial box, undercroft stair, and loading yard.
- First sublevel: Sacrifice Ward, reused as the active prisoner level.
- Arena Barracks connects the ward to the Old Work Gate.
- On grand-melee night, a passing Tyrian column camps outside for entertainment; it is not hunting escapees in advance.
