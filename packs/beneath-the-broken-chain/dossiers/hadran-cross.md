# Captain Hadran Cross

Captain of the Sacrifice Ward. Controls prisoner discipline, rations from the central table, work details, and movement to the Arena floor. He savors physical suffering and the moment someone betrays a value, identity, or loved one to make it stop. His calm is concentration, never restraint.

## Inventory

- Master keys
- Hooked mancatcher and weighted baton
- Private ledger naming victims, methods, dates, and buyers of torture viewings

## Relationships

- Jorad Pell values his control but would execute him if the private-viewing business became public.
- Malkor barely knows his name; Cross treats any distant praise as recognition.
- Targets people Korrik protects because Korrik will not ask for freedom.
- Wants to force Pauk to eat a person so the Arena can present every halfling as the same.
- Alters Mera Vonn's totals and uses Iltan Rusk's old slave contract against him.

## Abilities

- Studies what each prisoner protects, then telegraphs a single correction with counterplay: short cup, broken night, dull gear rack, reassignment, or false mercy.
- If publicly defied, hurts a helper or dependent. If his ledger is stolen, abandons routine security to hunt it.
- If defeated, grovels and trades keys, routes, Jorad's secrets, or reserve-gate orders, then seeks reversal.

## Quirks

- Uses each prisoner's full recorded name.
- Runs a thumb across his key-ring teeth while waiting for an answer.
- Calls coerced betrayal “an honest answer” and torture “helping the body tell the truth.”
- Laughs softly at involuntary sounds and asks the victim to repeat them.
