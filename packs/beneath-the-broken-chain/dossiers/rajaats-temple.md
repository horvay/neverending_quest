# Rajaat's Temple

Green Age temple Rajaat built above a preexisting Blue Age Ruin. It is not a halfling temple. Its Long Seal expired several weeks before the Campaign.

## Establishment

- Foundation Hall and floor-set Long Seal stair
- Threshold Gallery
- Hall of Measures
- Heat-seeking graft garden
- Seal Engine and research passages
- Material breaks exposing the older Blue Age Ruin

Evidence here shows captive experimentation, the Sacrifice Ward's original purpose, and Rajaat's later alteration of Rhulisti work. Rajaat remains imprisoned; nothing here is a live conversation with him.
