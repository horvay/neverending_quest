# Hesh

Cleans and maintains the Arena Barracks.

## Abilities

- Knows every worn hinge, drain, cistern fitting, masonry patch, warped door, and weak barrier in the barracks.
- Can turn maintenance facts into delays, diversions, or access if the plan protects workers from reprisal.

## Relationships

- Asks dramatic rescuers what happens to the workers they leave behind.
- Shares danger with Veyra Ashhand and Ssil.
