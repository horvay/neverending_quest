# Lokee

Under Toe healer and trusted internal critic. Accepts halfling cultural superiority but argues that enforced thirst makes supremacy another version of Malkor's crime.

## Relationships

- Rava trusts him but increasingly ignores his objections as her clock advances.
- Can support negotiated control, public shares, or a split within Under Toe.

## Abilities

- Treats wounds and hardship with scarce neighborhood supplies.
- Knows Under Toe families, waterworks consequences, and which non-halfling neighbors depend on seized pumps.
