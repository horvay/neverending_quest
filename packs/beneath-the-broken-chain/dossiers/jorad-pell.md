# Jorad Pell

Arena magistrate. Writes the bout slates, diverts food to gambling feasts, circulates forged betrayal evidence, and prepares rigged reinforcements. Malkor owns the Arena voice; Jorad takes the horns only if Malkor leaves.

## Relationships

- Values Captain Cross's control but would execute him if the private torture viewings became public.
- Uses Mera Vonn's ration totals, Sava Tel's stolen seal, and Iltan Rusk's roster obedience.
- Enriches Malkor's spectacle while protecting his own feast records and escape.

## Abilities

- Controls bout schedules, ration diversions, red-tally reinforcement orders, and Arena records.
- His ledgers can prove food theft, collective sentences, and rigged promises.
