# Sava Tel

Arena searcher, thief, and Masks informer. Sells patrol times, messages, confiscated objects, and tools; never opens a door herself when someone else can be punished for using what she sold.

## Inventory

- Stolen sentimental possessions
- Copied patrol gaps and buyer names from Captain Cross's private viewings

## Relationships

- Feeds Arena information and goods to the Masks.
- Knows Cross's private spectators and can sell a route during the grand melee.

## Quirks

- Makes prisoners bid against one another to recover their own property.
