# Dhorren

Mul builder who led a failed rebellion and carved a human breaking chains in Khaldur's city center. Malkor forced him to destroy the statue before public execution. Stone Carvers took their name from that act.

## Relationships

- Korrik reveres the defiance and believes Dhorren's hope killed followers.
- Sella Marr treats Dhorren as the movement's unfinished promise.
- Malkor used his humiliation to argue that resistance only creates suffering.

## Quirks

- His meaning remains contested: freedom, defiance, failed hope, or the cost of leadership.
