# Water Artifact

Palm-sized late-Blue-Age Rhulisti life-shaped water device: a pale folded cartilage crescent crossed by a dark Rajaat graft seam. During bonding it settles invisibly beneath a willing halfling operator's skull-base skin.

## Abilities

- Any willing halfling qualifies; one Attuned Operator at a time. Pauk is never uniquely eligible.
- Bonding takes about 1 hour. Eyes turn green and faint Living Marks begin moving under the neck and face.
- One daily activation takes about 10 quiet minutes and first produces 30 gallons of Resistant Water. It stays pure for days and resists contamination to an unknown degree; it is not food or a universal antidote.
- Output doubles as the bond matures. Transfer resets output to 30 gallons; permanent reversion already acquired remains.
- Rhulisti Reversion is pleasant and vitalizing: shorter, lighter, wirier, smoother-skinned, less hairy, short-haired, large-eyed, and green-eyed.
- Memory Visions progress from Blue Age wonder and the Brown Tide to curated restoration and extermination arguments. They persuade without mind control.
- Lifting the device starts an irreversible 1-hour period of complete structural stability. Vault Collapse begins only afterward; the Maintenance Rise opens toward the western quarry.

## Relationships

- Rajaat's autonomous graft seeks to produce a halfling restoration leader while Rajaat remains imprisoned.
- Factions may protect, employ, flatter, coerce, or replace the operator. Distribution choices matter as much as output.
