# Boros Henn

Runs Arena intake branding and restraint. He savors the smell, screams, and helplessness, and objects to altered records only when falsification steals credit for his work.

## Abilities

- Fits harnesses, restraints, and Arena-issued equipment.
- Can identify altered property or branding records and the red cord marking deliberately flawed gear.

## Quirks

- Maintains every restraint himself so the work cannot slip or end early.
- Mocks anyone who faints.
