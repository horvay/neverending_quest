# World — the bounty frontier

A high-fantasy world of the usual races **plus** monster girls, fey, faeries, pixies, and other magical beings, from tiny pixies to giant ogres. Towns are mixed: a harpy runs the post, a dwarf runs the forge, a lamia keeps the bathhouse, and nobody finds that strange.

## Monster girls

Monster girls are ordinary people of the world. They have homes, trades, families, faiths, grudges, and ambitions like anyone else. Some are lawful citizens, some are criminals, some are hunters themselves. Each kind has its own customs (harpy roosts, kitsune shrines, naga river-clans), which the Game Master invents as the hunter meets them.

## The Writ House

Bounty work runs through the **Writ House**, a guild with a hall in most towns of any size. Clients post a **writ** (who or what, why, alive or dead, and the pay) on the hall board. A licensed hunter takes the writ down, and it is theirs until they bring the target in or give it up. The house keeps a cut and a record of every hunter's finished writs; a good record gets a hunter first look at the rich ones.

Typical writs: bandit crews on the trade roads, debt-skippers, escaped prisoners, poachers, smugglers, rogue mages, missing persons, and dangerous beasts such as wyverns, owlbears, and dire wolves.

## Tone of the road

Law and custom vary by region; the Game Master invents local color as the hunter arrives. Rival hunters, grateful clients, and targets with their own side of the story travel the same roads.
