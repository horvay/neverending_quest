## Description

**Ren Caldew** — a lean dock runner out of Brinewatch. Wind-cut face, rope-burned hands, moves like someone who bills by the trip. Speaks little until a price is named.

## Inventory

- oilskin coat, patched at the left elbow
- empty tin flask
- coil of spare line (about four fathoms)
- belt knife, edge honest, not pretty
- three ceramic bits in a knotted rag

## Powers

### Salt-lung

Ren can breathe brine mist and shallow well-gas without choking. Does not grant water-breathing in open sea. The tell is a salt crust at the corners of the mouth after.

## Notes

- Starting state: hired muscle on the Brinewatch dock; attic pallet on tab at the Salt Lamp.
- Known faces: Mira Venn (innkeeper), Kell Reed (ferryman).
- Will take Grey Spit hops if Kell is willing and the weather is not a liar.
