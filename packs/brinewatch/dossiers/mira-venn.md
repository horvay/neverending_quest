# Mira Venn

Innkeeper of the Salt Lamp. Practical, dry humor, lemon crate behind the bar. Feeds dock runners on tab. Keeps the census in a book she does not show.

## Manner

Short orders. Watches hands, not speeches. Will call a fool a fool once.

## Relationships

- Puts Ren Caldew on the attic pallet when the dock is slow.
- Sends ferry errands through Kell Reed.
- Buys barrels from Jess Pike.
- Aunt Orren is her mother's sister; they argue about net-rot and nothing else.
