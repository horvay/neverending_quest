# Kell Reed

Ferryman of Brinewatch. Spare oar under the bench. Will not take the well path after dark. Grey Spit hops when the water allows.

## Manner

Few words. Names tides, not feelings. If he likes you he still charges.

## Relationships

- Runs Mira Venn's supply hops.
- Knows the brine-well is deeper than it looks.
- Will not discuss the Cinder Skiff in the Salt Lamp.
