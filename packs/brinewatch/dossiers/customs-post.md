# Customs Post

North piling. A shed, a desk, a stool, a rusted stamp-box with no stamps. No clerk in two winters. Holt Gann sits the steps because the roof still sheds rain.

## Notes

- Legal cargo does not move through here.
- The desk drawer sticks. No one admits to having opened it last.
- Dock Hands use the lee wall for a piss in weather.
