# Holt Gann

Tide-watcher, not a priest. Sits the customs-post steps with a slate and a cracked ear-horn. Knows fog-neap nights by the smell before the fog arrives.

## Manner

Patient, literal, slightly deaf on the left. Corrects "church" to "weather" every time.

## Relationships

- Tolerated at the Salt Lamp if he pays bits, not stories.
- Kell Reed will take his slate hop to Grey Spit.
- Aunt Orren says he was almost useful once.
