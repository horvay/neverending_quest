# Aunt Orren

Net-mender. Mira Venn's aunt. Lives in a lean-to off the net racks. Hands like teak. Smokes eel skin when she can get it from Grey Spit.

## Manner

Sharp, not unkind. Will mend your net and insult your knots in the same breath.

## Relationships

- Mira is "the girl with the book."
- Sells mended nets to Dock Hands.
- Claims the brine-well "took a boy in her mother's year." Will not name him.
