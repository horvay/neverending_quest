# Net Racks

Between the Salt Lamp and the brine-well. Poles, hooks, drying nets, the smell of old fish and tar. Aunt Orren's lean-to is on the back side.

## Notes

- The well path starts here.
- After dark the racks creak even without wind.
- Spare poles are communal until someone carves a name.
