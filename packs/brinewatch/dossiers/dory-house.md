# Dory House

Boat shed by Jess Pike's barrel stacks. Two dories on blocks, one that still floats, a third that is firewood pretending. Smells of shavings and wet oakum.

## Notes

- Jess works the hoop bench just outside.
- Dock Hands borrow tools and sometimes return them.
- The floating dory is not for strangers.
