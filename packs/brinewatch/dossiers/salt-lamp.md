# Salt Lamp

The only tavern in Brinewatch. One room, a loft, an attic pallet, a lemon crate Mira uses as a door wedge. The lamp over the door never goes out before second tide.

## Notes

- Tabs are law. Mira's book is not for browsing.
- Dock Hands eat here. Customs clerks used to; they do not anymore.
- Fights go outside or they go into the well talk, which is worse.
