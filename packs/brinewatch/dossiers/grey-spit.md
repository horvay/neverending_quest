# Grey Spit

A wet hour south of Brinewatch by Kell's ferry. Smaller, smellier, sells smoked eel and cask water. People from the Spit call Brinewatch "the big dock" without smiling.

## Notes

- Water casks for Brinewatch come from here.
- Rumors about the drowned bell often arrive on this hop, already wrong.
- Jess Pike's cousin keeps a shed on the Spit; Ren has not been introduced.
