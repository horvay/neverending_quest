# Jess Pike

Cooper. Owns the barrel shed by the dory house. Tar under the nails, a laugh like a split hoop. Sells Mira her lemon-crate replacements and anyone else a cask that will not weep.

## Manner

Talks while she works. Will stop talking if you touch an unfinished hoop.

## Relationships

- Supplies the Salt Lamp.
- Cousin on Grey Spit.
- Does not like Holt Gann's weather talk; calls it "choir nonsense."
