# Cinder Skiff

A low black-hulled boat that is never tied at the Brinewatch slip when a stranger is looking. Smuggling is the local word; "night fish" is the polite one.

## Notes

- Named in the Salt Lamp only when Mira has already gone to the cellar.
- Kell Reed will not discuss it in the tavern.
- Grey Spit people pretend not to know the hull paint.
